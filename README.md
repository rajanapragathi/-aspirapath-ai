# AspiraPath AI

## From Aspiration to Achievable

AspiraPath AI is a Generative AI-powered education and career guidance platform built using AWS PartyRock.

It helps students convert their career aspirations into personalized and achievable education and career pathways by considering their academic profile, skills, budget, available time, location, and other real-world constraints.

## Problem Statement

Students often know what career they want, but they may not know the exact skills, courses, certifications, projects, costs, timelines, or qualifications required to achieve their goal.

Generic career guidance may not consider individual constraints such as:

- Academic performance
- Current skills
- Education level
- Budget
- Study time
- Location
- Relocation limitations
- Career timeline

## Proposed Solution

AspiraPath AI analyzes the student's profile, career aspiration, and constraints and generates a personalized career roadmap.

### Workflow

Student Profile
→ Career Aspiration
→ Constraint Analysis
→ Career Feasibility
→ Skill Gap Analysis
→ Learning Roadmap
→ Projects & Certifications
→ Weekly Action Plan
→ What-If Career Simulation

## What-If Career Simulator

The What-If feature is the key innovation of AspiraPath AI.

It allows students to change real-world constraints and see how their career pathway changes.

### Example

Original Budget: ₹50,000

New Budget: ₹10,000

The AI can generate a revised pathway focused on:

- Free learning resources
- Low-cost courses
- Practical projects
- Portfolio development
- Alternative learning options

## Responsible AI

AspiraPath AI follows responsible AI principles including:

- Transparency
- Fairness
- Privacy
- Safety
- Human oversight

The system does not guarantee admission, employment, salary, certification, or career success.

Users should verify current fees, scholarships, deadlines, eligibility requirements, and institutional information through official sources.

## Privacy

The application should collect only information required for career planning.

Users should never be asked to provide:

- Passwords
- Bank details
- Government identification numbers
- Other unnecessary sensitive information
